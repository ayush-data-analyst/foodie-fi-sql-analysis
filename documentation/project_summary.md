# Foodie-Fi SQL Case Study

## Business Problem

Foodie-Fi is a subscription-based video streaming platform.

The objective is to understand customer behavior,
subscription trends, churn patterns, and upgrade
activities to support business growth.

## Analysis Performed

- Customer Acquisition Analysis
- Monthly Trial Distribution
- Churn Analysis
- Subscription Upgrade Analysis
- Plan Transition Analysis
- Customer Lifecycle Analysis

## SQL Techniques Used

- CTEs
- Window Functions
- Joins
- Aggregations
- Date Functions
- Ranking Functions

## Key Findings

- Total Customers: 1000
- Churn Rate: 30.7%
- Basic Monthly was the most common plan after trial.
- Average Upgrade Time to Annual Plan: 105 Days.
- No customers downgraded from Pro Monthly to Basic Monthly.

## Business Recommendations

- Reduce trial-to-churn customers through onboarding campaigns.
- Encourage faster migration to annual plans.
- Monitor churn behavior regularly.
